# Grill-Me

Relentless requirements interrogation and edge-case probing before coding or making architectural decisions.

## Objectives

- Interrogate user and system requirements before implementation begins.
- Probe boundary conditions, edge cases, error recovery, and concurrency concerns.
- Explore trade-offs and decision branches methodically.
- Reach complete shared alignment and record agreed decisions.

## Interrogation Areas

1. **Scope and boundaries:** What problem is being solved? What is explicitly out of scope?
2. **Edge cases and failures:** Empty inputs, scale limits, network failures, race conditions.
3. **Architectural trade-offs:** Performance vs simplicity, storage vs compute, maintenance overhead.
4. **Acceptance criteria:** Concrete, automated-test verifiable outcomes.
